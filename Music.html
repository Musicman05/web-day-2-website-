Patrick Star Endorses Tyler Anthony

Below is the music of Tyler Anthony, who Patrick Star endorses with his whole heart.

The Star is Tyler Anthonys first original released. A song he wrote when he was just 16 years old, its one that took 4 years to record and release. With the help of 2 studios in New Jersey he was able to get it recorded after 3 years. It was recorded once, but had numerous issues. So, in turn it sat dorment in his closet for 2 years. Then finally at 20 years he decided to go back to the studio and try once more. This time he got it right. The song was arranged by Steve Jankowski who also was the mixing, and audio engineer. With an instrumentation of Piano,Voice,Cello,Violin,Viola,and Acoustic Bass, there is a simple but heartwarming arrangment. Make sure to give it a listen, or else!
